## SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM (WHY 93% OF SMB LEADS DROP)
- **Baseline Data (5,000 Leads Cohort, Apr–Jun 2026):**
  - Only **368 out of 5,000 leads activate (7.36% conversion rate)**.
  - Steepest drop-off: **44% drop from Needs Assessed to Quote Accepted**.
  - Average turnaround: **17.6 days from lead to optical activation**.
  - Follow-up fatigue: **3–4 chase calls per customer** before router setup.
  - Blended Customer Acquisition Cost: **₹15,000 per activated business**.
- **Root Causes:**
  1. Sales reps pitch monthly targets, not customer needs.
  2. Quotes take 3–5 days as confusing PDF product codes.
  3. Non-GSTIN micro-enterprises drop out (only 2.0% activate vs 9.6% with GSTIN).
  4. Separate billing and support systems post-activation.

## SLIDE 5: INSTANT SMART KYC & DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE
*Answering Key Question #3: Instant verification while complying with DPDP Act 2023*
- **10-Second Digital Verification:**
  - Automated OCR parser extracts 15-digit GSTINs and Udyam MSME numbers.
  - Backed by instant lookup against 3,534 GSTIN records and 2,723 Udyam records.
- **DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Architecture:**
  - **Notice & Consent:** Pre-upload transparent consent modal declaring exact telecom purpose.
  - **Purpose Limitation:** Used strictly for telecom subscriber provisioning under DoT rules.
  - **Data Minimisation & Masking:** Sensitive identifiers (PAN/Aadhaar) masked in UI and logs.
  - **Retention Limits:** Dropped lead documents auto-purged after 90 days.
- **Planted Defect Resiliency:** Automatically catches expired bills (>90 days), cancelled GSTINs, and name mismatches.

## SLIDE 6: THE PARTNER CO-PILOT (HYBRID CHANNEL MODEL)
*Answering Key Question #4: What role existing field force and local channel partners play*
- **Digital Co-Pilot, Not Competitor:**
  - Digital handles routine 80% self-service flow; field partners handle high-value assisted exceptions.
- **Back-Office Dashboard for Unclosed Leads (Stretch Goal #2):**
  - Partners see leads that dropped out during the advisor or quote phase.
  - 1-click WhatsApp recovery link with pre-filled bundle carts.
- **Priority Calling Queue with Propensity Scoring (Stretch Goal #3):**
  - Leads ranked by machine propensity score (0–100) based on GSTIN validity and turnover.
  - Direct 1-tap phone dialer for rapid follow-up.
- **Assisted On-Site KYC Override:**
  - When OCR encounters blurry local documents or partnership deed discrepancies, partners conduct 1-click physical spot checks.
