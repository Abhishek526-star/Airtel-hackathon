### Second-by-Second Narration & Click Sequence

#### [0:00 – 0:25] SCENE 1: THE MSME ACQUISITION BOTTLENECK
- **Visual:** Split screen showing the Airtel SYNC hackathon theme and the baseline funnel graph.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Every quarter, Accel Business ingests 5,000 MSME leads. But today, only 7% activate. Why? Because the journey takes 17 days, costs ₹15,000 in acquisition cost, and requires 4 follow-up calls. Welcome to Project Udyam Digital — where we enable Indian small businesses to go digital in one sitting on their mobile phone."*

#### [0:25 – 1:05] SCENE 2: CONVERSATIONAL DISCOVERY & WHATSAPP BOT
- **Visual:** Open browser at `http://localhost:5173/advisor`. Select "Hindi" from the language dropdown, then toggle to "WhatsApp Bot Mode".
- **Action:** Tap microphone or click options: Micro Retail -> 3 Employees -> Yes POS -> ₹2,500 Budget.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Instead of waiting 5 days for a salesperson, the owner meets our AI Business Advisor. It supports 10 Indian regional languages. Owners can use our responsive web wizard or our authentic WhatsApp Business Bot with voice input. In under two minutes, it curates the Accel Micro Retail Connect bundle with clear monthly pricing and zero technical jargon."*

#### [1:05 – 1:35] SCENE 3: TRANSPARENT QUOTE & INSTANT SMART KYC
- **Visual:** Navigate to Quote page, show 24-month commitment discount (5% OFF), then proceed to Smart KYC (`/kyc`).
- **Action:** Accept DPDP Act 2023 Consent -> Click "Balaji Dental Care (Active GSTIN - K01)" demo test card -> Instant verification!
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"On the quote page, pricing is 100% transparent with telecom SAC codes and itemized 18% GST. For onboarding, our Smart KYC is fully compliant with the DPDP Act 2023. Our automated OCR verifies business credentials against official mock registries in under 10 seconds — unlocking non-GSTIN micro-enterprises through Udyam registration."*

#### [1:35 – 2:05] SCENE 4: ZERO-CALL TRACKER & B2B GST INVOICE DOWNLOAD
- **Visual:** Complete checkout -> Arrive at Order Lifecycle Tracker (`/order/ORD-2026-8921`). Click "Demo: Complete & Activate".
- **Action:** Live banner lights up with Circuit ID and Assigned Static IP. Click "Download Tax Invoice (PDF)" -> Real PDF opens!
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"No more calling support 4 times. The Live Order Tracker displays real-time provisioning milestones and the assigned field engineer. Once activated, the customer instantly downloads their official DoT and GST-compliant B2B Tax Invoice with cryptographic verification."*

#### [2:05 – 2:40] SCENE 5: FIELD PARTNER CO-PILOT & PROPORTIONATE CALLING
- **Visual:** Navigate to Partner Dashboard (`/partner`). Show the 4 KPI cards including "Advisor Drop-offs (120)".
- **Action:** Click "Priority Call Queue (Propensity)" -> Leads sort with highest scores first. Click "WhatsApp" recovery button.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Rather than displacing local channel partners, Udyam Digital acts as their digital co-pilot. Partners access unclosed leads dropped by the advisor, sorted by machine propensity score. With one click, the partner can call the owner directly or send a pre-filled WhatsApp recovery cart."*

#### [2:40 – 3:00] SCENE 6: BUSINESS IMPACT & CONCLUSION
- **Visual:** Navigate to Executive Analytics (`/admin`). Show the 5,000 leads cohort conversion and ARPU metrics.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"The impact for Accel Business? We slash activation time from 17 days to 24 hours, cut acquisition cost by 72%, and triple conversion from 7% to 22% — generating an additional ₹2.8 Crore in recurring revenue per cohort. Project Udyam Digital makes small businesses go digital in one sitting."*
